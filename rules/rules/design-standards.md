## Design & build — full-stack, modular, clean and on-brand
- Whenever you design or build something, cover BOTH sides — backend AND frontend — and deliver them together as one coherent whole, never just one half.
- Structure every design around a modular architecture and established best-practice patterns: clear separation of concerns, small reusable units, no monolithic dumping ground.
- Make the frontend and backend deliverables clean, professional and modern — production-grade, not throwaway.
- Match the existing system's visual identity: reuse its color palette, spacing and component style instead of inventing a new look.
- Keep examples and sample data realistic and sensible — never absurd, joke, or over-the-top placeholder content.
