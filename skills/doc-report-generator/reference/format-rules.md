# Format rules

Everything here is taken from `templates/writing-guide.docx` (Düzce University
graduate thesis writing guide, December 2022). The template in `templates/` already
encodes these rules in its styles, so a document built on it inherits them — this
page is for checking a document, or for rebuilding the layout somewhere else (HTML,
LaTeX, another template).

## Page

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Paper | A4 (21 × 29.7 cm) |
| Left margin | **3.5 cm** (binding edge) |
| Right / top / bottom margins | 2.5 cm |
| Text must not leave that frame | including figures and tables |

## Type

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Body text | Times New Roman, **12 pt** |
| Footnotes, table contents | 8–10 pt when needed to fit |
| Line spacing | **1.5** |
| Paragraph indent | none |
| Space between paragraphs | 6 pt before and 6 pt after — never a blank line |
| Alignment | justified |

## Headings

| Level | Appearance | Space before / after |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ALL CAPITALS, 14 pt, bold | 48 pt / 24 pt |
| 2 | ALL CAPITALS, 12 pt, bold | 24 pt / 12 pt |
| 3 | Title Case, 12 pt, bold | 12 pt / 6 pt |
| 4–6 | Title Case, 12 pt, italic | 6 pt / 6 pt |
| Front-matter headings | like level 1 | 6 pt / 6 pt |

A first-level heading always starts a new page. Front-matter headings (acknowledgements,
contents, lists of figures and tables, abbreviations, abstract) are set like first-level
headings but keep the 6 pt spacing.

## Figures, tables, maps

- Place them as close as possible after the paragraph that first mentions them.
- **Figure caption goes under the figure; table caption goes above the table.**
- Numbering is per chapter: `Figure 2.1.`, `Figure 2.2.`, `Table 3.1.` — the caption
  ends with a full stop.
- Leave 1.5 line spacing between the text and the figure, and between the figure and
  its caption; 12 pt after the caption before the text resumes.
- Multi-part figures are lettered: `Figure 2.3. View 3 a) Istanbul b) Eminönü c) Galata`.
- An item that spans two pages repeats its number and caption on the second page.
- Anything larger than A4 is reduced to A4 in the text and added full size as an appendix.

## Page numbers

| Section | Numbering | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Outer cover, inner cover, approval, declaration, acknowledgements, contents | counted, not printed | — |
| Lists of figures/tables, abbreviations, symbols, abstract, extended abstract | roman (i, ii, iii …) | bottom centre |
| Introduction onwards: method, findings, conclusions, references, appendices | arabic (1, 2, 3 …) | bottom centre |
| Curriculum vitae | not numbered | — |

## Quotations, equations, footnotes

- Quotations shorter than ~3 lines stay in the paragraph in quotation marks; longer ones
  are set as an indented block.
- Equations are numbered on the right in parentheses, per chapter: `(2.1)`.
- Footnotes are numbered per page and set 8–10 pt.
